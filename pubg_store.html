Where do I find my Player ID?

Open PUBG Mobile, tap your profile picture, and copy the number shown under your name.

Do I need to share my game password?

Never. We only need your Player ID. Do not send your password or login codes to anyone.

What is the bonus UC?

Some packs include extra UC on top of the base amount. It is shown after the plus sign on each pack.
